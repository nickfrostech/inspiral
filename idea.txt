# Inspiral Events - Core Creative & Technical Foundation

Colors:
- Canvas/White: Pure White (#FFFFFF) & Warm Alabaster (#FAF9F5)
- Botanical Greens: Deep Forest (#0E291E), Botanical Emerald (#1B4332), Soft Sage (#D8E2DC)
- Golden Yellows: Champagne Gold (#D4AF37), Radiant Ochre (#E5B842), Pale Gold Luster (#FDF8EC)

Typography:
- Display/Headlines: Dahlia (Art Nouveau botanical display serif) / Fallback: Cormorant Garamond
- Body/UI: Plus Jakarta Sans (clean, geometric, high readability)

Glass Effects:
- Refractive Liquid Glass using QuickLiquid (<LiquidGlass>, <LiquidTabBar>, liquidPress)
- Applied to: Navigation bar, Category tab switcher, floating hero CTAs, and media badge tags
- Desktop: Optical refraction with subtle chromatic dispersion (0.18) and Snell's law bezel bending
- Mobile fallback: CSS backdrop-blur-md with delicate 1px specular white borders for 60fps performance

Flowering & Organic Micro-Interactions:
- Flowering using: Framer Motion ('motion/react') SVG organic petal unfurl on hover & radial spore particle bursts on click
- Staggered blossoming reveals on page scroll (staggerChildren: 0.12s, ease: [0.16, 1, 0.3, 1])

Layout & Whitespace:
- Generous editorial whitespace (clamp-based padding py-24 to py-36)
- Asymmetrical luxury grids inspired by Kinfolk & architectural monographs
- Rounded hierarchy:
  - Buttons/Pills/Badges: rounded-full (9999px)
  - Cards & Content Blocks: rounded-3xl (24px to 32px)
  - Modals & Hero Frames: rounded-[2.5rem] (40px)

Tech Stack:
- Vite + React (JavaScript / JSX)
- Package manager: pnpm
- Tailwind CSS (configured with darkMode: 'class')
- motion ('motion/react') for physics-based spring animations
- Native Dark Mode from day one: Velvet Forest / Obsidian canvas with luminous emerald and glowing champagne gold
